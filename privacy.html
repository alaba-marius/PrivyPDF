Private by design. Proven by the network tab.

PrivyPDF reads your document into this tab's memory and handles it on your device, using engines that ship with the page. Nothing is uploaded. There is no backend that accepts files.

Open the editor, open DevTools, and watch the Network tab. Every request stays on this site. No request contains your document, and none goes to another server. The editor also counts requests to other origins. It should always read zero.

How to verify it yourself

You don't have to take our word for it. These steps take about a minute.

  1. Open the editor.
  2. Open DevTools (F12 or Ctrl+Shift+I / Cmd+Option+I) and switch to the Network tab.
  3. Check "Disable cache", then open or drop a PDF in the editor.
  4. Watch the Network tab. You may see requests for this site's own pages, styles and scripts. None go to any other server, and none carries your document.
  5. Close or reload the tab. Your file is gone; it lived only in this tab's memory.

The editor shows a privacy meter ("0 requests to other servers since you opened this file"). It should stay at zero.

Frequently asked questions

Are my files really never uploaded?

Yes. PrivyPDF has no backend that accepts files. Your document is read into this tab's memory and processed on your device. A strict CSP blocks every other origin.

Do I need an account?

No. There are no accounts, logins, cookies or trackers.

Does it work offline?

No. The pages load while you're online; after that everything runs in your browser on your device. There's no offline mode yet.

What happens when I close the tab?

Your file is gone. Files live only in this tab's memory and are discarded when you close or reload it.

Is there a file size limit?

Each file is limited to 500 MB. There's no page-count warning; big documents are limited by what your device's memory can hold.

Can I open password-protected PDFs?

No. PrivyPDF can't open or export password-protected PDFs.
